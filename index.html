About the Range

Shaping Focus.
Building Champions.

Bhopal Shooting Range is a premier destination for aspiring and competitive shooters in Bhopal. Our shared vision is to create a professional, disciplined and welcoming environment where shooters of all ages can develop their skills with confidence.

With a strong commitment to excellence, we focus on quality coaching, modern training methods and personalised guidance to help every athlete achieve their full potential. Bhopal Shooting Range has earned a reputation for nurturing beginners as well as competitive shooters with dedication, integrity and passion for the sport.

Range Owners — Jyoti Soni & Richa Raghuwanshi

Jyoti Soni and Richa Raghuwanshi are the proud owners of Bhopal Shooting Range. Under their leadership the range has grown into a disciplined, athlete-first training centre for the shooting community of Madhya Pradesh.

Professional Pistol & Rifle Shooting Coaching in Bhopal

Structured coaching across the full range of Olympic and ISSF pistol and rifle events — from your first pellet to national selection trials.

Olympic

10 m Air Pistol

The cornerstone precision event. Grip, stance, trigger release and shot-process discipline built from the ground up.

Men · Women · Mixed Team

Olympic

10 m Air Rifle

Standing rifle precision with emphasis on position building, natural point of aim and hold control.

Men · Women · Mixed Team

Olympic

25 m Rapid Fire Pistol

Five targets, shrinking time limits. Rhythm, recoil recovery and target-to-target transition under pressure.

Men

Olympic

25 m Sports Pistol

The precision and rapid-fire combination event, coached as two distinct skill sets inside one match plan.

Women

ISSF

25 m Standard Pistol

150, 20 and 10-second stages — timing discipline, consistent lift and a repeatable hold-and-release routine.

Men · Women

ISSF

25 m Centre Fire Pistol

Precision plus duel stages with centre fire calibre — recoil management and follow-through under match load.

Men

ISSF

50 m Free Pistol

The purest test of pistol marksmanship. Long-range sight picture, patience and shot-by-shot decision making.

50 m Rifle 3 Positions

Kneeling, prone and standing — position technique, equipment fitting and stamina across the full course of fire.

Head Coach

Rishi Soni

Rishi Soni is the Coach at Bhopal Shooting Range, bringing extensive competitive and coaching experience to the range. A highly accomplished pistol shooter and coach, he has earned recognition for his performances across multiple pistol disciplines and for his commitment to developing the next generation of shooting athletes.

From 2022 to 2025 he competed at national level in all ISSF pistol disciplines, establishing himself as a respected and well-known shooter within the Indian shooting community. He continues to participate in the Indian Team Selection Trials, demonstrating ongoing dedication to high-performance competition while mentoring aspiring shooters.

  • Indian Team Selection Trials — participant 2022 – present
  • National competitor for Madhya Pradesh 2021 – present
  • National-level competition in all ISSF pistol disciplines 2022 – 2025
