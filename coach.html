Rishi Soni is the Coach at Bhopal Shooting Range Academy, bringing extensive competitive and coaching experience to the academy. A highly accomplished pistol shooter and coach, he has earned recognition for his performances across multiple pistol disciplines and for his commitment to developing the next generation of shooting athletes.

From 2022 to 2025 he competed at national level in all ISSF pistol disciplines, establishing himself as a respected and well-known shooter within the Indian shooting community. He continues to participate in the Indian Team Selection Trials, demonstrating ongoing dedication to high-performance competition while mentoring aspiring shooters.

In the News

Press Coverage

“Rishi Soni awarded ISSF ‘A’ Grade coaching certification”

Translated from the original Hindi report

Rishi Soni, head coach of Bhopal Shooting Range Academy, has achieved a significant milestone in the field of shooting. He completed the International Shooting Sport Federation (ISSF) Academy’s ‘A’ level coaching course held in Delhi and received his certification. He had previously completed the ‘D’, ‘C’ and ‘B’ level courses.

During the course, Soni had the opportunity to train under international coaches and experts, and to share his own experience with them. At the academy he trains athletes in both pistol and rifle shooting, and under his guidance athletes have gone on to compete at state and national-level competitions.

The certificate was presented to him by NRAI President Shri K. N. Singhdeo.
